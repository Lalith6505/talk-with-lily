Privacy Policy for Talk with Lily: English Tutor

Effective Date: September 30, 2026
Developer: Lalith Madanayake (lmadanayake@gmail.com)

Core Commitment: Talk with Lily is a privacy-first, one-time paid app. We do NOT collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data, conversations, or account information to developer servers. The app uses a Bring-Your-Own-Key (BYOK) architecture where your private API keys and chat history stay exclusively on your device.

1. Information We Collect and Process

Talk with Lily does not operate private backend servers, analytics services, or user tracking infrastructure.

  • No User Account: You do not need to register, log in, or provide personal details (name, email, phone number) to use the app.
  • No Telemetry or Analytics: The app contains zero advertising SDKs, zero user tracking scripts, and zero diagnostic telemetry.
  • Local Storage Only: All conversation settings, selected difficulty levels, and user API keys are stored solely on your device inside Android's private SharedPreferences container.

2. Bring Your Own Key (BYOK) Architecture

Talk with Lily connects directly from your device to third-party AI model providers using the credentials you provide:

  • Supported providers include Anthropic (Claude), Groq, OpenAI, and Google (Gemini).
  • When you send a message, your conversation prompt is transmitted directly from your device to the official endpoint of your chosen provider via encrypted HTTPS.
  • Your API keys and conversations are never routed through, stored on, or accessible by our servers or any third-party intermediary.
  • Your interactions with each AI provider are subject to that respective provider's terms of service and privacy policy.

3. Device Permissions & Data Usage

  • Microphone (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO): Used only when you tap the microphone button to practice spoken English. Audio is converted into text via the Android operating system's built-in Speech Recognizer. Audio recordings are never retained, stored, or uploaded to any server.
  • Internet (android.permission.INTERNET): Used exclusively to communicate with the user-selected AI API endpoints and D-ID video service.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Because all data resides strictly on your local device:

  • You can wipe your chat history, API keys, and preferences at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings > Apps > Talk with Lily > Storage > Clear Data.
  • Uninstalling the app immediately and permanently purges all local data from your device.

5. Children's Privacy

Our app does not knowingly collect or solicit any personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. The app is rated for Everyone and operates completely client-side.

6. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
